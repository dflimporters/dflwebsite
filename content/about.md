## About Us

## Our Story

DFL's journey began in 1976 as a livestock farm located 20 miles outside of Kingston.  What started as a family-run operation soon grew into one of the largest egg producers on the island. Every product from the farm was directly supplied to leading supermarkets, restaurants, and hotels.
In 1995, fueled by our unwavering dedication to employees, customers, and products, the DFL team expanded into the distributorship business.  Maintaining our focus and passion, DFL now operates from three strategically positioned warehouse facilities.  Our distribution network spans every major town, with dedicated representatives covering the entire island.

## Our Mission

Building one positive relationship after another at every step of the business cycle, through our team and our products and services.

## Our Vision

Becoming the most trusted Company in the region, by staff, customers, suppliers, and consumers.
